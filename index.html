Giving that reaches the right hands

Sevadeep is a digital platform that connects donors with verified NGOs. People give furniture, clothes, appliances and household goods, and orphanages, old-age homes and other social organisations receive what they actually need.

What Sevadeep does

In-kind donations

Donors see what NGOs need and give goods in place of money, so help matches real requirements.

Verified NGOs only

Every recipient organisation is screened first, so only genuine social organisations are onboarded.

Pickup and tracking

For donors in Pune, a volunteer collects the item from the door, and the donation is followed until it is received.

Narayan Seva kitchen

A food programme that serves nutritious meals to relatives of patients at Sassoon Hospital, Pune.

Who it helps

Orphanages, old-age homes, learning centres and other community organisations, along with the families of patients who depend on the Narayan Seva kitchen for a daily meal.

How you can contribute

Donate an item

Clothes, books, furniture, appliances or utensils you no longer need.

Sponsor meals

Pay for meals at the Narayan Seva kitchen through UPI.

Volunteer

Offer your time and skills for pickups, teaching, sorting or tech help.

Share surplus stock

NGOs can list spare supplies and urgent needs to help one another.

About the team behind it

Sevadeep is an initiative of the Relfor Foundation, the philanthropic arm of Relfor Lab, and is based in Pune, Maharashtra.
